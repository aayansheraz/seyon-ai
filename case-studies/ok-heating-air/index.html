CS.04 — OK Heating & Air

Building a Digital System That Keeps the Phone Ringing Year-Round

Residential & Commercial HVAC

Website DesignSEOAI Search Optimization (AEO)CRM IntegrationLead AutomationLanding PagesAI Implementation
Key Outcome
"A digital system designed to support growth long after launch."

The Opportunity

For most HVAC companies, the busiest days come when customers need help immediately.

Whether an air conditioner stops working in the middle of July or a furnace goes out during a cold front, homeowners aren’t spending days researching contractors. They’re looking for someone they trust, someone who answers the phone, and someone who can help quickly.

OK Heating & Air already had the experience and reputation to earn that trust. The challenge was making sure their online presence reflected the quality of their work.

Their website provided basic information about the company, but it wasn’t designed to consistently generate service calls or make it easy for potential customers to take the next step.

Our goal wasn’t simply to redesign the website; it was to create a system that helped the business attract more qualified leads, improve search visibility, and simplify how new inquiries were handled from the moment a customer reached out.

Understanding the Challenges

When we looked at the existing customer journey, a few opportunities became clear.

The website wasn’t organized around the services homeowners search for most often. Visitors could find information, but they often had to work harder than they should to locate the service they needed or request an appointment.

The company also had limited visibility for many high-intent searches, making it harder for customers to discover the business organically.

Like many growing service companies, new inquiries were handled manually, creating additional administrative work and making response times more dependent on how busy the office happened to be.

None of these challenges were uncommon, but together they created unnecessary friction for both the customer and the business.

seyon.ai’s Approach

Rather than thinking of the project as “building a website,” we focused on building a better customer experience from beginning to end.

Every decision was centered around one simple question:

"How can we make it easier for someone with an HVAC problem to become a customer?"

That philosophy guided every stage of the project.

Website Strategy

When someone’s heating or air conditioning stops working, they don’t want to dig through complicated navigation or search endlessly for a phone number.

The website was redesigned to help visitors find answers quickly and contact the company with as little effort as possible.

We reorganized the navigation around the company’s core services, created dedicated pages for heating, cooling, maintenance, repairs, and installations, and made calls-to-action more visible throughout the site.

Mobile performance was also a major priority since many homeowners search for HVAC services from their phones during an unexpected breakdown.

The result was a cleaner website focused on helping visitors move from searching to scheduling without unnecessary obstacles.

SEO & AI Search Optimization

Having a great website means very little if customers never see it.

After the new website was built, we focused on improving how the company appeared in both traditional search engines and AI-powered search experiences.

Rather than filling pages with keywords, we organized content around the questions homeowners naturally ask.

Topics like AC repair, furnace replacement, seasonal maintenance, indoor air quality, emergency HVAC service, and energy-efficient upgrades were each given dedicated pages with helpful, relevant information.

Behind the scenes, we improved metadata, internal linking, structured page hierarchy, and schema markup to help search engines better understand the website while making the content easier for AI search platforms to reference.

CRM Integration

A faster response often means the difference between winning or losing a customer.

Every contact form, service request, and estimate inquiry now feeds directly into the company’s CRM, giving the team one centralized place to manage incoming leads.

Instead of sorting through emails or manually entering customer information, new inquiries are organized automatically, allowing the team to respond more efficiently.

Automation

One of the biggest opportunities was reducing the manual work that happens after someone submits a request.

To accomplish that, we built automated workflows that immediately notify the team when a new inquiry comes in while also confirming to the customer that their request has been received.

Additional internal workflows help organize leads, reduce repetitive tasks, and keep the process moving without requiring constant manual oversight.

The goal wasn’t to automate customer relationships; it was to remove repetitive work so the team could focus on helping customers.

AI Implementation

AI was introduced thoughtfully throughout the project where it could improve efficiency without replacing the personal service that homeowners expect.

Whether supporting communication, organizing information, or improving internal workflows, every implementation was chosen because it solved a real operational challenge.

Technology should make running a business easier—not more complicated.

The Outcome

By the end of the project, OK Heating & Air had more than a redesigned website.

The business now has a digital system designed to support growth long after launch.

Customers can quickly find the services they need, request appointments more easily, and receive a smoother experience from the very first interaction.

Behind the scenes, CRM integration and automation help the team manage new opportunities more efficiently while reducing repetitive administrative work.

The result is a stronger online presence built not only to attract more visitors but to convert those visitors into qualified service calls.

CS.06 — Horizon Dental Care

Creating a Digital Experience That Makes Choosing a Dentist Feel Simple

General & Cosmetic Dentistry

Website DesignSEOAI Search Optimization (AEO)CRM IntegrationOnline Appointment RequestsLead AutomationAI Implementation
Key Outcome
"A digital platform that reflects the quality of care patients experience in the office."

The Opportunity

For many people, choosing a dentist isn’t a decision they make lightly.

Whether they’re looking for a routine cleaning, cosmetic treatment, or a new provider for their family, patients want to know they’re choosing a practice they can trust. Before scheduling an appointment, most people spend time reading reviews, comparing offices, exploring services, and getting a feel for the practice online.

Horizon Dental Care had built a reputation for providing high-quality care and creating a welcoming environment for patients of all ages. While that experience was evident inside the office, it wasn’t fully reflected in the practice’s digital presence.

The website contained the information patients needed, but it didn’t always make that information easy to find. Online appointment requests required additional manual follow-up, and there was an opportunity to improve how the practice appeared in both traditional search results and emerging AI-powered search experiences.

The objective wasn’t simply to launch a new website.

It was to create a digital experience that made prospective patients feel comfortable, simplified scheduling, and provided the practice with a stronger foundation for long-term growth.

Understanding the Challenges

Before beginning the project, we looked at the practice from the perspective of someone searching for a new dentist.

Most prospective patients have similar questions.

  • Do they accept new patients?
  • What services do they offer?
  • What does the office look like?
  • Can I schedule online?
  • Will I feel comfortable bringing my family here?

The existing website answered many of those questions, but not in a way that naturally guided visitors toward scheduling an appointment.

Service information was grouped, making it harder for patients to quickly find what they were looking for, and the office relied on several manual processes to manage new patient inquiries.

While none of these issues prevented the practice from delivering excellent care, they created opportunities to improve both the patient experience and internal efficiency.

The seyon.ai Approach

Rather than approaching this as a simple website redesign, we focused on building trust before a patient ever walked through the front door.

Every page, every call-to-action, and every workflow was designed around one guiding question:

"How can we make scheduling a first appointment feel as easy and comfortable as possible?"

That became the foundation for the entire project.

Website Strategy

The website was redesigned to create a clean, welcoming experience that reflected the atmosphere patients would find in the office.

Navigation was simplified so visitors could quickly find information about general dentistry, cosmetic procedures, preventive care, emergency appointments, and new patient resources.

Dedicated pages were created for each major service, giving patients a clearer understanding of available treatments while improving search visibility.

We also placed greater emphasis on trust-building elements throughout the site, including provider biographies, patient testimonials, office photography, insurance information, and answers to frequently asked questions.

Clear calls-to-action were incorporated throughout the website, making it simple for visitors to request an appointment without feeling overwhelmed.

Because many patients search from their phones, the website was designed with a mobile-first approach to ensure scheduling remained convenient across all devices.

Search Visibility & AI Search Optimization

A modern website only creates value if prospective patients can find it.

Following the redesign, we focused on improving the practice’s visibility in both traditional search engines and AI-powered search platforms.

Instead of creating broad pages about dentistry, we developed dedicated content around the services patients commonly search for, including routine cleanings, dental implants, crowns, cosmetic dentistry, teeth whitening, emergency dental care, and family dentistry.

Behind the scenes, we implemented metadata optimization, structured page hierarchy, internal linking, schema markup, and AI-friendly content organization to help search engines better understand the website.

More importantly, each page was written to answer common patient questions in plain language rather than technical dental terminology.

CRM Integration

Providing a great patient experience begins long before someone arrives for their appointment.

Every appointment request, contact form, and new patient inquiry now flows directly into the practice’s CRM, giving the office one centralized location to manage communication and scheduling.

Instead of relying on scattered emails or manual tracking, staff can quickly organize inquiries, follow up with prospective patients, and maintain a more consistent scheduling process.

Automation

We also identified several opportunities to simplify routine administrative work.

Automated appointment request confirmations reassure patients that their inquiry has been received, while internal notifications allow staff to respond more efficiently.

Additional workflows help organize new patient information, reduce manual data entry, and create a smoother scheduling experience from beginning to end.

Automation wasn’t introduced to replace personal interaction.

It was designed to eliminate repetitive tasks so the staff could spend more time caring for patients.

AI Implementation

AI was implemented carefully and only where it could provide meaningful value.

Rather than replacing conversations between patients and staff, AI was used to improve operational efficiency, organize information more effectively, and support everyday workflows behind the scenes.

Every implementation served a practical purpose while maintaining the personal experience patients expect from their dental provider.

The Outcome

By the completion of the project, Horizon Dental Care had more than an updated website.

The practice now has a digital platform that reflects the quality of care patients experience in the office.

Visitors can easily learn about services, meet the providers, request appointments, and find answers to common questions through a clean, organized experience designed around patient confidence.

Behind the scenes, CRM integration and automation have streamlined appointment requests and reduced administrative work, giving the team more time to focus on patient care.

The result is a stronger online presence that supports sustainable growth while creating a better experience from the very first interaction.
